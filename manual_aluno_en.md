# Student Manual - MIPS (Interactive Room Map)

## 📚 Welcome to MIPS

MIPS is a digital platform developed to assist in managing and navigating IPS rooms. This manual will help you use all features available for students.

---

## 🔐 How to Login

1. Access the MIPS login page
2. Enter your registered **email**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click the **"Login"** button
5. You will be redirected to the main student page

> **Note:** If you have access issues, contact the system administrator.

---

## 🗺️ Available Features

### 1. View Interactive Map

This feature allows you to view the IPS room map.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Ver Mapa"** (View Map) in the top menu
2. The 2nd floor map will be displayed automatically
3. Use the **side arrows** (◀ ▶) to navigate between floors:
   - **Right arrow:** Go to Floor 3
   - **Left arrow:** Back to Floor 2
4. **Hover your mouse** over any room to see additional information
5. **Blue markers** indicate room locations

**Map Features:**
- Interactive visualization of floors 2 and 3
- Room markers with informative tooltips
- Simple navigation between floors
- Responsive design (works on mobile and tablet)

---

### 2. Search Room and Open Map

This is the fastest way to find a specific room.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Procurar"** (Search) in the top menu
2. In the **"Selecione a Sala"** (Select Room) field, type the room name/number (e.g., F210, F315)
3. A suggestion list will appear as you type
4. Click on the desired room or press **Enter**
5. The map will automatically open on the correct floor
6. The chosen room will be **highlighted in yellow** for easy identification

**Tips:**
- You can type only part of the name (e.g., "F2" shows all rooms on floor 2)
- The system automatically adjusts for uppercase/lowercase
- Available rooms: F210, F211, F212, F212A, F212B (Floor 2) and F311, F313, F315, F317, F319, F314A, F316, F318, F320 (Floor 3)

---

### 3. Account Information

View your account and profile data.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Informações da Conta"** (Account Information) in the top menu
2. You will see the following information:
   - **Account type:** Student
   - **Full name**
   - **Registered email**
   - **Description of features** available for students

---

### 4. Logout

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Sair"** (Logout) in the top menu
2. You will be redirected to the login page
3. Your session will be securely closed

---

## 📱 Mobile Usage

MIPS is fully responsive and works perfectly on mobile devices.

**Mobile Menu:**
1. Click the **menu icon** (☰) in the top right corner
2. The menu will expand showing all options
3. Select the desired feature
4. The menu closes automatically after selection

**Mobile Tips:**
- Use your fingers to zoom the map (pinch-to-zoom)
- Navigation arrows are easily clickable
- All boxes are centered for better viewing

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I book rooms?**
A: No, only teachers have permission to make room reservations.

**Q: How do I know which room I have class in?**
A: Use the "Search" feature to quickly find your room location.

**Q: Does the map work offline?**
A: No, you need to be connected to the internet to access MIPS.

**Q: Can I see security alerts?**
A: No, this feature is only available to security members.

**Q: I forgot my password, what do I do?**
A: Contact the system administrator to recover access.
