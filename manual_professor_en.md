# Teacher Manual - MIPS (Interactive Room Map)

## 📚 Welcome to MIPS

MIPS is a digital platform developed to assist in managing IPS rooms. This manual will help you use all features available for teachers, including room reservation management.

---

## 🔐 How to Login

1. Access the MIPS login page
2. Enter your registered **email**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click the **"Login"** button
5. You will be redirected to the main teacher page

> **Note:** If you have access issues, contact the system administrator.

---

## 🗺️ Available Features

### 1. View Interactive Map

View the IPS room map with all available spaces.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Ver Mapa"** (View Map) in the top menu
2. The 2nd floor map will be displayed automatically
3. Use the **side arrows** (◀ ▶) to navigate between floors
4. **Hover your mouse** over any room to see:
   - Room name
   - Reserved hours (if applicable)
   - Last security alert
5. **Blue markers** indicate available rooms
6. **Red markers** indicate rooms with active alerts

**Tooltip Information:**
- **Room name**
- **Reserved hours today:** List of occupied times
- **Last alert:** Door opening/closing information

---

### 2. Search Room

Quickly find any room on the map.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Procurar"** (Search) in the top menu
2. Type the room name/number (e.g., F210, F315)
3. Select the room from the suggestion list
4. The map will automatically open on the correct floor
5. The chosen room will be **highlighted in yellow**

---

### 3. Reservation Management

View, create, and cancel room reservations. This is one of the most important features for teachers.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Salas Livres"** (Free Rooms) in the top menu
2. You will see a table with all your reservations:
   - **Sala (Room):** Reserved room number
   - **Professor:** Your name
   - **Data (Date):** Reservation date
   - **Hora (Time):** Reserved time
   - **Ações (Actions):** Buttons to cancel

**Cancel a Reservation:**
1. Locate the reservation in the table
2. Click the **"Cancelar"** (Cancel) button (❌ icon)
3. Confirm the action in the confirmation window
4. The reservation will be removed immediately

**Important Information:**
- You can see all your active reservations
- Reservations are organized by date and time
- You can only cancel your own reservations
- Cannot edit a reservation (must cancel and create new)

---

### 4. Make New Reservation

Reserve a room for a specific date and time.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Pedir Sala"** (Request Room) in the top menu
2. Fill out the form with:
   - **Sala (Room):** Select from list (e.g., F210, F315)
   - **Data (Date):** Choose desired date
   - **Hora (Time):** Select time (24h format, e.g., 14:00)
3. Click **"Reservar"** (Reserve)
4. The system will verify:
   - If the room exists
   - If the date is valid
   - If there's already a reservation for that room at that time
5. You will receive confirmation or error message

**Reservation Rules:**
- Must be a valid time (8am-8pm)
- Cannot reserve already occupied times
- Date must be future or today
- A room can only have one reservation per time slot

**Possible Messages:**
- ✅ "Reserva efetuada com sucesso!" (Reservation successful!)
- ❌ "Já existe uma reserva para esta sala neste horário" (Room already booked at this time)
- ❌ "Por favor preencha todos os campos" (Please fill all fields)
- ❌ "Sala inválida" (Invalid room)

---

### 5. Account Information

View your account and profile data.

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Informações da Conta"** (Account Information) in the top menu
2. You will see:
   - **Account type:** Teacher
   - **Full name**
   - **Registered email**
   - **Description of features** available

---

### 6. Logout

**How to use:**
1. Click **"Sair"** (Logout) in the top menu
2. You will be redirected to the login page
3. Your session will be securely closed

---

## 📱 Mobile Usage

MIPS is fully responsive and works perfectly on mobile devices.

**Mobile Menu:**
1. Click the **menu icon** (☰) in the top right corner
2. The menu will expand showing all options
3. Select the desired feature
4. The menu closes automatically after selection

**Mobile Tips:**
- Use your fingers to zoom the map
- Reservation tables have horizontal scroll
- All forms are optimized for touch
- Boxes are centered for better viewing

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many reservations can I make?**
A: No limit, but respect available times and don't make duplicate reservations.

**Q: Can I reserve a room for someone else?**
A: No, reservations are always registered in your name.

**Q: Can I edit an existing reservation?**
A: Not directly. Must cancel old reservation and create new one.

**Q: How do I know if a room is free?**
A: Check the map and hover over the room to see reserved hours.

**Q: Can I make recurring (weekly) reservations?**
A: No, each reservation must be made individually.

**Q: When can I cancel a reservation?**
A: Anytime before the reserved time.

## 📊 Best Practices

✅ **Plan ahead:** Reserve rooms with enough time
✅ **Cancel unused reservations** to free up the room
✅ **Check availability** on map before reserving
✅ **Confirm details** (room, date, time) before submitting
✅ **Logout** when finished, especially on shared computers

---

## 🔔 Notifications

The system does **not** send automatic reservation notifications or reminders. It's your responsibility to:
- Remember your reservations
- Regularly check the reservation list
- Cancel reservations you won't use
